# iRoya funding proposal

29 October 2026

## Proposed investment

NGN 2,410,000 for 10% fully diluted ownership immediately after investment.
Implied post investment valuation: NGN 24,100,000.
Implied pre investment valuation: NGN 21,690,000.
This is a proposed commercial offer, not an independently verified valuation.

## Twelve month budget

| Cost | Annual NGN |
|---|---:|
| Staff member one at NGN 25,000 monthly | 300,000 |
| Staff member two at NGN 25,000 monthly | 300,000 |
| Vercel Pro at USD 20 monthly | 360,000 |
| Supabase Pro from USD 25 monthly | 450,000 |
| Additional operations | 1,000,000 |
| Total | 2,410,000 |

Hosting uses a planning rate of NGN 1,500 per USD, one deploying Vercel seat and one Supabase production project. This is twelve months of hosting, not a quoted one off deployment labour fee. Founder technical oversight and the existing software are assumed available.

## Suggested operations allocation

- Domain email data and general administration: NGN 180,000
- Hotel onboarding and visits: NGN 250,000
- Promotion and sales materials: NGN 250,000
- Minor maintenance and support tools: NGN 150,000
- Operating buffer: NGN 170,000

These allocations total NGN 1,000,000 and may be reassigned within that ceiling. They are planning allocations, not supplier quotes. New hardware, a founder salary, major redevelopment, statutory employment costs, tax and externally charged deployment labour are not separately funded. Cloud overages, extra seats, taxes and FX charges can increase costs.

## Launch plan

Complete release gates and production checks in month one. Recruit three pilot hotels in months two and three. Target ten active hotels by month six and fifteen paying hotels by month twelve, conditional on pilot conversion and retention. No current customer or revenue claim is implied.

## Commercial test

Test a subscription of NGN 15,000 per hotel per month. At Paystack local pricing of 1.5% plus NGN 100, each collected subscription leaves NGN 14,675 before tax and other variable costs. Fourteen paying hotels leave NGN 205,450 monthly after those collection fees, compared with the average monthly budget of NGN 200,833.33. This is a cost coverage illustration, not a profitability forecast. Booking transaction fees are separate. No commission revenue is assumed.

## Investment terms

New capital is proposed to enter the company for newly issued shares. Existing holders collectively retain 90% immediately after closing, subject to confirming the cap table and any other rights. Final share class, investor rights, company approvals and software ownership must be established in definitive documents. Future rounds may dilute ownership. Financial returns are not guaranteed.
